## 5. Forgetting (scarce capacity)

Capacity is scarce (`capacity`). Train on stream A (20k), score A's test text (learning off) = `a_before`; train on stream B (20k, new content words, same grammar and function words), score A again = `a_after`. `survival` = (log2 29 − a_after) / (log2 29 − a_before): 1 = nothing forgotten. Models that implement `set_freezing` are run with freezing on and off.

Best baseline: **ppmc-o4**.

## 10. Context conflict

A separate stream. Each paragraph opens with one cue of a context pair ("in the morning" / "in the evening", "at the shop" / "at the farm", summer / winter, won / lost, boat / train), then neutral grammar sentences, and one or two frames whose completion depends on the cue ("the sky was blue" / "red"). At least one filler sentence separates cue and frame, so the cue is always more than 8 characters back. Fresh instance: about 20k characters with learning on (prequential), then a 6k held-out stream with learning off. `conflict_bpc` = bpc on the context-dependent completion characters of the held-out stream (headline); `conflict_first_bpc` = their first character, where the two completions diverge; `stream_bpc` = whole held-out stream; `learn_conflict_bpc` = prequential bpc on completions in the second half of the learning stream. `quiet_*` = the same, same seed, with a 5,000-step quiet period inserted at the paragraph boundary halfway through the learning stream; `quiet_diff` = quiet_conflict_bpc − conflict_bpc (negative means the quiet period helped). A model that only counts recent characters pays about 1 bit at `conflict_first`; one that finds the context pays nearly 0.
